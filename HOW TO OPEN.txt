DUKHAN LOUNGE · website preview

How to open
1. Unzip the folder (if it came as a .zip).
2. Double-click "index.html". It opens in your browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox).
3. Keep everything in this folder together, the page needs the css, js and assets folders next to it.

Good to know
- Best viewed on a laptop or desktop. It also works on phones.
- Move your mouse over the first screen to see the smoke.
- The Google Map and the live football fixtures need an internet connection.
  Offline, the page still works and shows a sample match schedule.
- Prices, flavours and menu items are placeholders for now, send changes and they can be updated.
